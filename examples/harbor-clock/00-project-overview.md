# 港钟停在三点十七分

## Premise

父亲去世后，档案员陆余回到即将拆除的海港钟楼，发现父亲曾参与篡改一场旧救援的公开人数。她必须决定是替父亲保留一个体面的沉默，还是让一份并不完整的记录重新被看见。

## Delivery Contract

- Planned chapters: 4
- Target chapter length: 750-1000 Chinese characters
- Intended reader effect: first feel the seduction of a clean explanation, then accept a public record that leaves human guilt and怯懦在场。
- Draft state: release candidate after revision pass 01
- Required delivery files: all control files, four chapter cards, four chapters, first-draft review, revision ledger, and quality report

## Story Rules

- Genre: contemporary coastal realism with a restrained mystery structure.
- Promise: the mystery is about how a community edits memory, not about finding a villain.
- Ending direction: the clock is removed; a public table replaces a private myth.
- Taboo boundaries: do not turn grief into spectacle; do not treat testimony as certain proof; do not resolve the mother-daughter relationship with forgiveness language.
