# First-Draft Review

## Cold Reader

- Ch1 reached its premise quickly, but the first version told the reader what Lu Yu thought about her father before the clock tower had become a tangible place.
- Ch3 made the notebook feel like a prize delivered at the correct moment. The scene needed a cost that explained why the mother kept it.
- Ch4 gave emotional closure more quickly than the public-record theme allowed.

## Continuity Editor

- The clock must remain a stopped time reference, not proof that a particular person caused the rope failure.
- Lu Cheng’s signature is established; his intent is not. Later prose must retain that distinction.
- Xu Fan’s uncle can be documented as absent, but no source supplies a reason.

## Line Editor

- Remove abstract moral conclusions where a tool, paper, or interrupted answer can carry the pressure.
- Let dialogue deflect through work details before it exposes motive.
- Reduce repeated salt-and-rust imagery in the final chapter.

## Required Repairs

The four repairs are recorded as R1-R4 in `09-literary-revision.md`; each was re-read in its surrounding chapter after revision.
