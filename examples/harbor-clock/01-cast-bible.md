# Cast Bible

## Lu Yu

- Role: protagonist, municipal archive digitizer, 31.
- Surface: exact, quiet, dislikes unfinished forms.
- Hidden pressure: her father taught her that a neat record can keep a family alive; she fears discovering that his neatness was cowardice.
- Desire: know what happened at 03:17 without becoming the person who ruins her mother's remaining shelter.
- Arc: from searching for one clean verdict to choosing a useful but incomplete public record.

## He Xiaozhen

- Role: Lu Yu's mother, retired harbor canteen worker, 58.
- Surface: practical, keeps surplus keys and salt in labeled jars.
- Hidden pressure: she asked Lu Cheng to keep quiet when the family needed the relief allotment.
- Desire: protect the ordinary life built after the storm.
- Arc: from hiding the watch notebook to allowing it to be copied with its gaps intact.

## Xu Fan

- Role: clock repairer and Lu Yu's former school friend, 32.
- Surface: jokes when asked for certainty.
- Hidden pressure: his uncle is named in the rescue notebook as absent from a shift.
- Desire: keep the clock tower from becoming a public accusation machine.
- Arc: from refusing to help to helping publish a record with annotation rather than erasure.

## Lu Cheng

- Role: deceased harbor clerk and Lu Yu's father.
- Stable fact: he signed the altered public rescue count.
- Unknown: whether he initiated the alteration or submitted under pressure.
