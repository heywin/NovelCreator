# Chapter 01 Card

## Purpose

Bring Lu Yu to the demolition-bound clock tower and make the stopped time feel personal before it becomes a mystery.

## Entry State

Lu Yu arrives with a municipal camera case on the morning the demolition notice is posted.

## Required Beats

- The clock face reads 03:17.
- He Xiaozhen provides an old tower key but avoids explanation.
- Lu Yu finds a notebook cover whose pages have been removed.

## Character Constraints

Lu Yu does not accuse her mother. He Xiaozhen speaks through household instructions.

## Relationship Pressure

The old key creates cooperation without trust.

## Timeline And Setting Constraints

2026-08-08 morning; demolition begins in two days.

## Plot Line Movement

Open the stopped-clock and hidden-record lines.

## Foreshadowing And Payoff

The empty notebook cover returns when the torn pages surface in Ch3.

## Narrative Boundary

Keep all planning and evidence labels out of the manuscript. Show pressure through keys, paper, metal, silence, and work.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not identify a culprit or explain Lu Cheng’s motive.

## Exit State

Lu Yu decides to inspect the tower before it is removed.

## Post-Draft Update Targets

Record the tower key, the cover, and the 03:17 observation.
