# Style And Voice

- POV: close third person through Lu Yu.
- Diction: concrete work objects, salt air, paper, metal, domestic storage; avoid scenic excess.
- Dialogue: people hedge, interrupt, and use practical details to avoid confession.
- Rhythm: short observation before emotional inference; allow failed searches and silences.
- Banned habits: verdict-like moral summary, melodramatic confession, decorative weather that explains emotion.
- Narrative boundary: manuscript stays inside the town and never names planning, evidence labels, chapter functions, or writing process.
